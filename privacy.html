Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how the Audiobook Player Android app (package com.kendis.voicebookreader, the "app") handles information. The app is published by Kendis, an individual developer ("I", "me").

In short: the app does not collect your personal data. Your library, listening progress, bookmarks and statistics stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • Your library: titles, authors, cover images and the location of the audio files you add (the app reads title, author and cover from the files' own metadata).
  • Listening progress, chapters, bookmarks and the notes you write.
  • Listening statistics: how long you listened, the day and hour, and the playback speed, used for the statistics screen and the optional CSV export.
  • Your settings, such as theme, skip intervals and daily goal.

I do not receive, see or copy any of this. It is never sent to me or to any server run by me. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Files you choose

The app opens audio files and folders only through Android's system file picker, so it can access just the items you select. It does not request broad access to your storage, photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera.

The app also uses technical permissions that do not give access to personal data: keeping playback running in the background (wake lock, foreground service), showing the playback notification, checking whether a network connection exists, and restoring the home-screen widget after the device restarts.

Internet access

The app has the internet permission for two purposes: (1) downloading an audio file from a web address that you type in "Download from URL", and (2) communicating with Google Play for purchases. Nothing else is sent.

Purchases and subscriptions

The optional "Pro" subscription is sold and processed entirely by Google Play through the Google Play Billing Library. Payment details are handled by Google and are never available to me or to the app. The app only learns whether your Google account has an active Pro subscription, so it can unlock Pro features. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications, widget and Android Auto

  • Notifications: a media notification shows the current book and playback controls. You can deny the notification permission.
  • Home-screen widget: shows the current book's title, author and cover on your home screen.
  • Android Auto: if you connect the app to Android Auto or a similar car system, the titles and authors in your library are shown on the car display so you can choose a book. This happens through Android and the car system on your device; the app does not send the data anywhere else.

Sharing, selling and third parties

I do not sell or share personal information, because I do not collect any. The app contains no advertising networks, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services. The only third-party service involved is Google Play (see above).

Exporting your data

The statistics screen can export your listening history as a CSV file. The file is written to the location you pick on your device. Where it goes afterwards is up to you.

Data retention and deletion

Everything is stored on your device until you remove it. You can remove books inside the app, or delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app in Android settings. Because I hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
